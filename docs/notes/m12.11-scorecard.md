is a single-trial observation rather than the planned three-trial scorecard.

M12.9, M12.10 or any other slice.

## Guard observation

Only `django__django-10914` was cut by a guard. It stopped on
`stalled_test_failures_unshrunk` at turn 57, after the visible failing-test set
did not shrink across three test runs, and it still resolved. No guard touched
any failure. All other stops were either a normal `end_turn` or the 75-turn
outer cap.

## Sympy finding

`sympy__sympy-11897` was not an empty-patch case. It produced a non-empty
529-byte patch and applied it, changing `is_constant()` to `is_Rational` in
`sympy/simplify/radsimp.py`, then reached the 75-turn cap and failed the
official harness. This differs from the prior restricted baseline, where sympy
produced an empty patch twice. It is a wrong-patch/max-turns failure, not a
premature-finish cut; `premature_finish_*` did not fire.

`apply_patch` succeeded once and failed twelve times: seven hunk bodies exceeded
their header counts, three hunk lines were malformed, one patch had a context
mismatch, and one had a missing or invalid hunk header. Agents generally fell
back to `edit`. This is a single-run observation about tool reliability, not a
causal comparison.

All ten final patches applied cleanly and no whitespace failure was recorded.
The `apply_patch` failures above are tool-call failures before the final
`git diff` patch was produced; they are separate from the final patch check.

## Limits

- One trial, not three. There is no pass@3 and no trial-to-trial spread.
- Ten instances from a fixed bring-up subset. The 30% to 90% interval is the
  honest resolution of that subset, not a Lite-wide result.
- No control arm. The measured rate and the behavioural counts are observations,
  not evidence that any wave-1 or wave-2 change caused a gain.
- The prior restricted baseline is a separate run, not a contemporaneous
  control.
- Requests is environment-invalid; the 6/10 denominator includes it.
